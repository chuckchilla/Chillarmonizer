1  Welcome

Chillarmonizer packs the classic studio harmonizers, harmony and pitch pedals, and vocal harmony plugins into one plugin. Feed it a voice or an instrument and it can:

  • add up to four harmony voices, each thickened into a choir of up to four copies;
  • choose harmony notes from a fixed interval, a key and scale, a MIDI chord, MIDI notes, or chords played on a sidechain track;
  • bend pitch with an expression pedal, drop-tune, wobble with an LFO, freeze a sound and correct pitch;
  • add polyphonic octaves, a tracking synth, pitch-shifting crystal echoes and shimmer reverb.

Everything is in one window with four pages: HARMONY, PITCH FX, OCTAVES & FX and OUTPUT & MIDI.

Monophonic vs polyphonic. The "smart" features (Diatonic, the chord modes, pitch correction, the Formant engine and the tracking synth) follow one note at a time. They work best on vocals, solo lines and melodies. Octaves, Drop tuning, Bends and the Poly engine with Fixed Interval also work on chords.

2  Installation

Download

Get the latest build from the Chillarmonizer GitHub repository, under Releases → "Chillarmonizer (latest build)". Download the zip for your system.

Windows

  1. Unzip Chillarmonizer-Windows-VST3.zip.
  2. Copy Chillarmonizer.vst3 into C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\.
  3. Open your DAW and rescan plug-ins.

macOS

  1. Unzip Chillarmonizer-macOS-VST3-AU.zip.
  2. Copy Chillarmonizer.vst3 into ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/.
  3. Copy Chillarmonizer.component into ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/.
  4. Rescan plug-ins in your DAW. Logic and GarageBand use the Audio Unit.

Look for it under the manufacturer CHILLplugins. Its category is "Fx / Pitch Shift". On a Mac the Audio Unit is a MIDI-controlled effect, so it can receive MIDI notes for the MIDI modes.

Routing MIDI and the sidechain

  • MIDI: in most DAWs, create a MIDI track and set its output to the Chillarmonizer instance on your audio track. In Logic, choose the "MIDI-controlled Effects" version and pick the MIDI source in the Side Chain menu at the top of Logic's plugin window.
  • Sidechain: Chillarmonizer has an optional stereo sidechain input. Send the guitar or keys track to it using your DAW's sidechain selector. It is only used in Sidechain Chord mode.

3  Quick start

A diatonic harmony in under a minute

  1. Put Chillarmonizer on a vocal or a lead guitar track.
  2. Choose the preset Diatonic 3rd Up from the preset menu.
  3. On the HARMONY page, set Key to the key of your song, or choose Auto and let it detect the key from what you play.
  4. Pick a Scale. Major and Natural Minor cover most songs.
  5. Play or sing. The display shows the note it hears, and the keyboard lights the harmony note.
  6. Turn on Voice 2 and set its Degree to 5th up for a three-part triad.

Choosing the right engine

Tip. Set Input so the level meter peaks around two-thirds. If the note readout flickers, turn Tracking down. If it's slow to pick up quiet notes, turn it up.

4  Signal flow

INPUT ──► Input gain ──► Pitch tracker (note, key) SIDECHAIN ──► Chord detector │ │ │ │ ▼ ▼ │ Harmony brain: Mode + Key/Scale + MIDI + Sidechain chord ─┘ │ │ ├──► Drop/Raise + Bend + Correction ──► DRY path (shifted if needed) │ ├──► Voices 1–4 (× Choir copies): interval, detune, delay, pan, │ formant, glide, vibrato, humanize, HarModulator ├──► Octaves (Sub 2 / Sub 1 / Up 1 / Up 2) + Tracking Synth ├──► Freeze ▼ HARMONY bus ──► Crystal Echoes ──► Shimmer Reverb │ Dry level + Harmony level ──► Stereo mode / Width ──► Output gain ──► OUTPUT

Dry is your original signal. It is pitch-shifted only when you use Drop/Raise, a Bend targeting "Everything", or Pitch Correction. Harmony is everything Chillarmonizer adds. Set the two levels separately: turn Dry down to hear only the effect, for example on a send.

The On switch bypasses the effect with a short crossfade, so switching never clicks.

7  Harmony modes in depth

Fixed Interval

Each voice is always the same distance from your note, set with Interval. Perfect intervals (5ths, 4ths, octaves) sound right in any key. Thirds and sixths will sometimes clash with the key. This is the classic Whammy and PS-6 behaviour, and it works with chords on the Poly engine.

Diatonic (Key)

Harmonies stay inside the chosen Key and Scale. A "3rd up" is sometimes a major third and sometimes a minor third, depending on where your note sits in the scale, just as a backing singer would choose. If you sing a note outside the scale, the harmony is worked out from the nearest scale note.

Common Degree settings: 3rd up (+2), 5th up (+4), 6th up (+5), Octave (+7), 4th down (−3), 6th down (−5).

MIDI Chord

Hold a chord on a MIDI keyboard. Each voice moves to the chord tone that is Degree chord-tones above or below your sung note. The harmony follows the chord changes even when the melody note stays the same. With no chord held, the voices are silent.

MIDI Notes

Each MIDI note you hold is given to a voice, lowest note to Voice 1, next to Voice 2 and so on, up to four. The voices sing exactly those notes, re-pitching your input. This is how you "play" a choir from a keyboard (preset MIDI Choir (Play Keys)). Turn on as many voices as notes you want.

Sidechain Chord

Chillarmonizer listens to the sidechain input, works out which chord is being played (major, minor, 7, maj7, m7, dim, sus2, sus4) and harmonises the main input using those chord tones. Typical use: a guitar track on the sidechain steering harmonies on a vocal. The detected chord is shown in the display.

For best results, use a clean, clearly strummed or picked part on the sidechain. Heavy distortion and bass-only parts are hard to read.

8  Shifting engines

EngineHow it worksStrengthsLimits
Formant (Vocal)Pitch-synchronous overlap-add (PSOLA), driven by the pitch tracker.Natural voices; formants preserved and adjustable; Formant knobs active.One note at a time. About 25 ms latency.
Poly (Chords)Four overlapping 70 ms grains.Handles chords and complex sounds; smooth.Slight chorus or smear on large shifts.
Low LatencyTwo grains sized to whole periods of the detected pitch (8–30 ms).Tight feel for live playing and bends.Best on single notes.
Vintage 910Two 35 ms grains with near-hard splices.Classic glitchy, gritty harmonizer sound.Deliberately imperfect.

The engine applies to the harmony voices, Bends, Drop/Raise and Correction. Octaves always use their own polyphonic shifters.

Pitch tracking. The tracker covers about 60 Hz to 1.5 kHz, roughly a low B on a bass guitar up to a high soprano. That covers voices, guitar, bass and most lead instruments.

13  Presets, A/B and Morph

Factory and user presets

Choose presets from the header menu or step through them with < / >. Click SAVE and name your sound to store it as a .chillarm file in Documents/Chillarmonizer Presets. Your presets appear below the factory list. You can copy the files to another computer or share them.

Your DAW also saves the full state, including both A/B slots, with the project.

A/B slots

Chillarmonizer holds two complete settings, A and B. Click A or B to edit that slot. A>B copies A into B as a starting point for a variation. Use the slots to compare two ideas, or to set up a verse sound and a chorus sound.

Morph

Morph blends between slot A and slot B. Every continuous knob (levels, intervals, mixes, times) glides smoothly. Switches and menus (mode, engine, key and so on) are not morphed: they keep their current setting, so set them the same in both slots. Put Morph on an expression pedal (CC 4) or automate it to move from a dry verse to a full choir chorus in one motion.

15  Recipes

Backing vocals from a lead

Mode Diatonic, Engine Formant. Voice 1: 3rd up, pan −40 %. Voice 2: 5th up, pan +40 %. Choir 2, Spread 40 %, Humanize 30 %. Lower Harmony to −6 dB so the lead stays on top.

Low octave "bass singer"

Mode Fixed. Voice 1: Interval −12, Formant −3, pan centre, Level 70 %. Add Glide 60 ms for smooth note changes.

Dual-guitar harmony lead

Mode Diatonic, Engine Low Latency. Voice 1: 3rd up, pan right. Dry pan stays centre; use Stereo: Split if you run two amps.

Whammy-style pedal rig

Bend Target Everything, Engine Low Latency, Heel 0, Toe +12. Expression pedal on CC 11. Dry at 0 dB, voices off.

Verse-to-chorus lift

Slot A: one quiet voice. Click A>B, then in B turn on four voices, Choir 3 and some shimmer. Automate Morph from A to B at the chorus.

Ambient swell machine

Octaves Sub 1 50 %, Up 1 60 %, Attack 0.9 s. Reverb Mix 45 %, Decay 85 %, Shimmer 50 % at +12. Freeze on CC 64 for drones.

Keys play the choir

Mode MIDI Notes, all four voices on, Engine Formant, Dry −60 dB. Hold chords on a MIDI keyboard while singing a single sustained note.

Rhythmic canon

Mode Diatonic. Voice 1: 3rd up, Beat 1/8D, pan left. Voice 2: 5th up, Beat 1/4, pan right. Add a little Crystal Echo.

16  Troubleshooting

ProblemTry this
Harmony sounds warbly or jumps notesRaise Input so the meter is healthy. Lower Tracking. Make sure the source is a single note; for chords, use Fixed Interval with the Poly engine.
Harmony is in the wrong keySet Key by hand instead of Auto, and check the Scale. Auto needs a few seconds of playing to settle.
No sound from MIDI modesCheck that MIDI reaches the plugin (the display keyboard marks held notes). In Logic, use the MIDI-controlled effect version. Make sure the voices are On.
Sidechain Chord does nothingEnable the sidechain input in your DAW and send the chord track to it. Use clean, mid-register chords.
Formant knobs do nothingThey only work with the Formant (Vocal) engine.
Feels late when playing liveSwitch to the Low Latency engine. Formant adds about 25 ms; Poly adds a little more.
Echoes or reverb run awayLower Echo Feedback or Shimmer. High feedback with large Pitch values builds up fast.
Clicks when switchingUse the On switch or Morph rather than removing the plugin; both crossfade.
Plugin not found after installCheck the install folder (chapter 2) and rescan. On macOS you may need to restart the DAW.
